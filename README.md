# Why I chose this article #
This article is titled *How to Earn a Billion Dollars* written by Paul Graham who is an American computer scientist widely known for co-founding the seed capital firm Y-Combinator. It's central thesis is that it's not hard to make a billion dollars if you achieve compounding growth month over month. And I think it resonates with the very nature of computer software because software is meant to be distrubted at a large scale at no extra cost. With brick and mortar businesses expanding requires exponentially more cost as you grow, however with startups they can maintain an exponential trajectory without exponential cost.
